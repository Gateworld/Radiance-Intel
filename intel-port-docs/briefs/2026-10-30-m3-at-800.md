## Faults this run

- H77's rotation was heat-stopped at Tctl 76 C, the first heat stop since run 90. intel-host now idles at 41-42 C, above
  note 89's 40 C. The kill left bcs CAT errors on both cards (m3 faults 30 / 31; kill artifacts the runner does not
  count).
- On card1 the copy queue stayed stuck. The next step (BK12b) waited behind it until its timeout. Its kill reset card1
  (m3 fault 32, the same pattern as faults 28 -> 29). Card1 recovered, and its kernel log is clean.
- **Lesson:** after a heat stop's kill, run a small step before a long one.
